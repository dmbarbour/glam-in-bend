Mutations of the heap's growth (spike/kprim.bend's grow_more and the
writes it makes), each naming the laws of glam/GROWN_LAWS.bend it
should fail (docs/Proving.md; the gate runs scripts/one_law.py
--mutants on this file). Witnesses: a store of 32 words, eight nodes,
its capacity word 8, a tracked node at word 28.

two, so that it lands on word 31 of the old store: the tracked node at
word 28 reads its word 31 changed
--- ../spike/kprim.bend

  free(wr(h, (((wcap - 1 : U32) << 1n : U32) + 1 : U32), fh0), n)
