=== node_in_place nodes_keeps
each template node made in place at slot 0, not taken from the free
list: the header's words 0 and 3 written, word 3 outside the list (one
node, a lift, its payload word not 0, so that no later node writes word 3
back)
--- splice.bend

+++
      sp.put(sp.node(False{}, h, 0, t, by), map, i)
