Mutations of the code the loop's laws follow (glam/ring.bend's
R.situate), each naming the laws of glam/LOOP_LAWS.bend it should fail
(docs/Proving.md; the gate runs scripts/one_law.py --mutants on this
file). Each is caught by its law's proof failing: a heap with a free list
and a leaf laid out apart is costly to build as a witness.
