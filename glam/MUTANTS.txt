# Mutations of glam's code, each a fault a law of glam/LAWS.bend named
# should catch (scripts/one_law.py; docs/Proving.md, What to claim):
#   python3 -I scripts/one_law.py glam/PROOF.bend LAW --mutants glam/MUTANTS.txt
#
# Unlinking a cursor from its ring (unlink_keeps): each mutation writes a
# word the law says is left alone.

=== unlink_word2 unlink_keeps
the predecessor's word 2, its position, given the cursor's next
--- materialize.bend
  P.wr(h, (p + 3 : U32), nx)
+++
  P.wr(h, (p + 2 : U32), nx)

=== unlink_clears_own unlink_keeps
the cursor unlinked has its own word 3 cleared too
--- materialize.bend
  rg.unlink.set(rg.next(h, x), p)
+++
  P.wr(rg.unlink.set(rg.next(h, x), p), (x + 3 : U32), 0)
