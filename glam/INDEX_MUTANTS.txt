Mutations of a copy's index (glam/index.bend) and of its model's lookup
(glam/index_model.bend), each naming the laws of glam/INDEX_LAWS.bend it
should fail (docs/Proving.md; the gate runs scripts/one_law.py --mutants
on this file).

=== scan_31 ne_found
the scan of two keys' bits stops a bit short, so that keys differing
only at bit 31 seem alike
--- index.bend
  ix.crit(32n, Cr{0n, True{}}, a, b)
+++
  ix.crit(31n, Cr{0n, True{}}, a, b)

=== scan_same_flipped ne_found
a scan that stops where the keys agree
--- index.bend
  cr.k(ix.same(ix.side(a, i), ix.side(b, i)), i)
+++
  cr.k(Bool.not(ix.same(ix.side(a, i), ix.side(b, i))), i)

=== descent_wrong_side find_tree
a descent taking the child the key's bit does not give
--- index.bend
      ds.c(P.rd(h, ix.kid(ix.side(k, i), x)))
+++
      ds.c(P.rd(h, ix.kid(Bool.not(ix.side(k, i)), x)))

=== descent_fuel_32 find_tree
a descent one step short, so that a tree of 32 bits on its way ends
in an error
--- index.bend
def ix.fuel() -> Nat:
  33n
+++
def ix.fuel() -> Nat:
  32n

=== found_any find_tree
a find handing back whatever leaf it reaches, the key unchecked
--- index.bend
  ix.found.s(U32.is_eq(key, k), h, x)
+++
  ix.found.s(True{}, h, x)

=== empty_unread find_tree
a find on an index whose situating node points to itself going down
as though to a tree
--- index.bend
  ix.find.k(U32.is_eq(root, s), h, root, k)
+++
  ix.find.k(False{}, h, root, k)

=== look_wrong_side look_at find_tree
the model's lookup going to the other child, so that it misses the
keys present
--- index_model.bend
      mb.pick(X.ix.side(k, i), tr.look(r, k), tr.look(l, k))
+++
      mb.pick(X.ix.side(k, i), tr.look(l, k), tr.look(r, k))

=== side_unchecked look_at
the invariant no longer placing a branch's right keys on their side
--- index_model.bend
(tr.all(r, tr.first(l), i, True{}) && (tr.ok(l, 1n+i) && tr.ok(r, 1n+i)))))
+++
(Bool.or(tr.all(r, tr.first(l), i, True{}), True{}) && (tr.ok(l, 1n+i) && tr.ok(r, 1n+i)))))

=== rm_sibling_same_side remove_tree

not its sibling

      rm.found(P.rd(h, ix.kid(Bool.not(sd), cur)), loc, cur)
+++
      rm.found(P.rd(h, ix.kid(sd, cur)), loc, cur)

=== rm_root_kept remove_tree

=== rm_loc_stale remove_tree
