# Blend: the kernel moved onto it

Reviewed by the maintainer, 2026-11-04, as one plan with Blend's own,
which since lives with Blend (`blend/docs/plans/Blend_2026-11-04.md`,
`blend-own-repository`): this one keeps the kernel's part. Blend is our
preprocessor of static objects for Bend (`blend/README.md`, its guide
`blend/GUIDE.md`;
Codegen_2026-11-04.md, `codegen-vehicle.blend`): `.blend` files in,
`.bend` files out, the objects gone. This plan moves the kernel's
families onto it, measured against the templates they replace.

## What it serves

The kernel's chain from a pair to its rewrite is written once and
compiled once per strategy family through Bend's templates: 51 of
`spike/knet.bend`'s signatures carry its hooks (`~note`, `~fin`, `~wd`,
`~after`, `~erased`) and its types (`~S`, `~R`), as do the 400 generated
rewrites of `spike/krules.bend`; `spike/kstrat.bend` names a family's
hooks at each call into it, and writes a loop per family where a
template cannot recurse. A hook added threads through every signature;
a helper given a second caller splits a segment off every family's
chain. With Blend, a family is an object, its hooks definitions it
gives, its chain its own copy; an affine definition with two callers is
an error before any build. No law imports the kernel's modules
(`spike/LAWS.bend` imports the rule table, the store, the primitives),
so the transition touches none.

## Steps

### What the kernel needs of it

14. `blend-generated-objects` — tentative — the generated rewrites as
    members of an object every family inherits, `gen_krules.bend` writing
    a `.blend` where it writes a `.bend`: each family then has its own
    rewrites calling its own hooks, as the templates give it now. Until
    then the families pass their hooks to KR's templates as now.

### The kernel

15. `blend-kernel-chain` — planned — the family chain of knet.bend
    (`interact.f` down to `go.ext.r`, the dispatch, the interpreted
    table's path, `wd`, static erasure, the events) moved to
    `spike/knet.blend`: an abstract object declaring the hooks, a family
    object for each strategy, kstrat.bend calling `Demand.go`,
    `Eager.interact`, and so on, by those names; the interpreted table
    copied with each family, as now. One family first, plain demand,
    measured, then the rest. Acceptance: the library's every count and value alike;
    `sharing.py compare` showing each family's chain in segments of its
    own name and none more; the four builds against main within their
    noise or better; no hook left in a signature.
    - `blend-kernel-chain.slice` — built 2026-11-07 — found 2026-11-04:
      demand's `after` is itself a template argument of kstrat.bend's
      walk (`wk.rw(~after, ...)`; plain and collecting demand pass
      different hooks), so a family's `after` can be its own definition
      only once the walk is in the same `.blend`. The first slice: plain
      demand's walk with the chain beneath it, an object for each demand

      the other families on knet.bend's templates meanwhile, the chain
