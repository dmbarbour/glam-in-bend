Mutations of the store's growth (spike/kmem_grow.bend's KM.grow), each
naming the laws of spike/GROW_LAWS.bend it should fail (docs/Proving.md;
the gate runs scripts/one_law.py --mutants on this file). Witnesses: a
store of four words of 7, two levels deep, its capacity 4.

=== copy_from_1 grow_old
the copy starting at index 1, so that word 0 of the grown store is the
zero fill's, not the old word
--- kmem_grow.bend
      MG{copy(U32.to_nat(cap), 0, CP{a, [0 : U32^Nat.add(depth(32n, cap, 0n), 1n)]})}
+++
      MG{copy(U32.to_nat(cap), 1, CP{a, [0 : U32^Nat.add(depth(32n, cap, 0n), 1n)]})}
>>> witness grow_old
w = [7 : U32^2n]
d = 2n
cap = 4
j = 0

=== fill_ones grow_new
the grown store filled with 1, so that its new half's nodes read in use
--- kmem_grow.bend
      MG{copy(U32.to_nat(cap), 0, CP{a, [0 : U32^Nat.add(depth(32n, cap, 0n), 1n)]})}
+++
      MG{copy(U32.to_nat(cap), 0, CP{a, [1 : U32^Nat.add(depth(32n, cap, 0n), 1n)]})}
>>> witness grow_new
w = [7 : U32^2n]
d = 2n
cap = 4
j = 4

=== depth_two grow_full
the store grown two levels deeper, not one
--- kmem_grow.bend
      MG{copy(U32.to_nat(cap), 0, CP{a, [0 : U32^Nat.add(depth(32n, cap, 0n), 1n)]})}
+++
      MG{copy(U32.to_nat(cap), 0, CP{a, [0 : U32^Nat.add(depth(32n, cap, 0n), 2n)]})}
>>> witness grow_full
w = [7 : U32^2n]
d = 2n
cap = 4
